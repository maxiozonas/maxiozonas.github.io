# Technology marks

Original technology marks are stored locally to avoid runtime CDN requests.

- Most SVGs: [Devicon](https://github.com/devicons/devicon), MIT (see LICENSE-devicon.txt).
- Claude, OpenCode, Leaflet and Cloudinary: [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), CC0; OpenAI is from the 15.20.0 tag.

The marks belong to their respective owners and are used to identify technologies. Monochrome marks invert in the dark theme; coloured marks retain their original colours.
